Keep the best shot. Clear the rest.

KeepOne is a camera-roll cleaner for Android. It finds similar photos, bursts, screenshots, and large videos on your phone, suggests a keeper, and moves the rest to the system trash — after you confirm.

Get help Privacy policy
  • Everything stays on the phone

    Scanning, grouping, and scoring happen on the device. KeepOne has no server and no KeepOne account. Your photos and videos are never uploaded.

  • Nothing leaves without your OK

    Android shows its own confirmation before anything is moved. Items go to the system trash for about 30 days, so you can restore them from your gallery, Files, or Recently trashed in KeepOne. Space is freed when the trash is emptied or those items expire.

  • A real free cleanup first

    Scanning and reviewing are always free. You can move up to 750 items to the trash each calendar month. Pro only removes that monthly cap.

What KeepOne looks for

KeepOne groups near-duplicate shots and bursts, lists screenshots, and flags videos of 50 MB or larger. In a similar group it suggests a keeper using what’s already on the phone — for example whether a shot is marked as a favorite, how sharp it looks, and a simple on-device face-confidence score. You can keep every frame if you want. KeepOne does not claim to make your phone faster; it helps you free storage once the trash is emptied.
