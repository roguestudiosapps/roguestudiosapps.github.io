The app

KeepOne is a camera-roll cleaner. It runs on your phone (Android 11 and later), scans the photo and video library you allow it to read, groups similar shots and bursts, lists screenshots and large videos, and lets you move items you choose to Android’s system trash. We grant you a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the app on devices associated with your Google account, in line with Google Play’s rules.

We may update the app. Some updates are required for continued use or for purchases to keep working. Features can change. KeepOne does not claim to speed up your phone; it helps you free storage after items leave the trash.

Your library, and your responsibility

You decide what to move or delete. KeepOne never silently removes items. Android shows a system confirmation before a batch is trashed or permanently deleted. If you cancel that prompt, nothing moves.

When you confirm a trash request, items go to the system trash for about 30 days. You can restore them from your gallery, the Files app, or Recently trashed in KeepOne (that in-app list only includes items this install of KeepOne trashed). Space is freed when the trash is emptied or the items expire. Delete now in Recently trashed permanently deletes the selected items and cannot be undone. Android will ask you to confirm again.

KeepOne does not back up your photos or videos. Before you trash anything you might miss, use a backup you already trust (for example Google Photos or a copy on a computer). You are responsible for those choices. Similarity grouping is helpful but not perfect; review a group before you confirm.

Free plan and Pro

Scanning and reviewing are always free. On the free plan you can move up to 1,000 items per calendar month to the trash. The count resets on the first day of the month, in your phone’s time zone. If a batch is larger than you have left, KeepOne may offer to move the largest items that still fit, or to see Pro. Pro removes the monthly cap. That is the only difference. You still confirm every batch, and photos still stay on the phone.

The free monthly count is stored on the device. Reinstalling the app can reset it.
